\section{What the measurements support}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Our system is \emph{causal}: it consumes the current and past frames only ($t{-}4\ldots t$).
        OccAny's published configuration consumes the evaluated frame plus four \emph{later} frames
        ($t\ldots t{+}40$ raw frames, about $4$\,s of ego motion).
  \item \textbf{Occupancy.} In the single-camera setting our causal system exceeds OccAny on
        Occ3D-nuScenes ($+10.98$ raw) and is indistinguishable from it on SemanticKITTI
        ($-0.04$, CI spanning zero) \emph{while using no future frames}.
  \item \textbf{Semantics.} Against OccAny's own causal configuration we are ahead on SemanticKITTI
        ($4.45$ vs $3.97$). OccAny's semantic advantage over us exists only with the future window.
  \item \textbf{The future window is worth more to semantics than to geometry} for OccAny:
        $+1.91$ SC IoU but $+3.34$ SSC mIoU.
  \item Being causal costs \emph{our own} system $2.77$ SC IoU on identical targets.
  \item Not claimed: absolute state of the art; superiority in OccAny's six-camera surround setting
        (it leads there); or any significant gain from our learned semantic head over the trivial
        nearest-evidence baseline (see \S\ref{sec:caveats}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Protocol definitions}

All numbers use OccAny's own samples, labels, grid placement and evaluator unless stated.

\begin{description}\itemsep2pt
  \item[Track O] every voxel with $\text{label}\neq255$ on the $256\times256\times32$ grid. OccAny's
        convention; all comparison tables below are Track O.
  \item[Track P] $\text{valid}\cap\text{horizontal FOV}\cap\text{range}\le40$\,m. Our own analysis
        convention, \emph{not} comparable to OccAny. Reported once, in \S\ref{sec:ours}.
  \item[causal window] frames $t{-}4\ldots t$. Our deployed setting.
  \item[forward window] frames $t\ldots t{+}40$ (stride 10). OccAny's published setting; also run on
        our system to match their protocol.
  \item[geometry pooling] $3\times3\times3$ majority \emph{dilation} of occupancy. Changes SC IoU.
  \item[semantic pooling] $3\times3\times3$ majority \emph{relabelling} of already-occupied voxels.
        Leaves SC IoU unchanged. Both are the default of OccAny's released metric script
        (\texttt{USE\_MAJORITY\_POOLING=1}, \texttt{POOLING\_MODE=separate}) and are not described in
        their paper.
  \item[SSC mIoU] OccAny's \texttt{SSCMetrics.get\_stats}: mean IoU over all 19 non-empty classes,
        absent classes counted as $0$.
\end{description}

\section{Caveats and open items}\label{sec:caveats}

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep2pt
  \item \textbf{Frame budgets differ in the causal semantic comparison.} We use 5 causal frames; OccAny's
        causal configuration uses 1. OccAny's 5-frame \emph{causal} number is not measured (see
        \S\ref{sec:retracted}), so we have strictly more input in that row.
  \item \textbf{Our learned semantic head is not shown to help.} The frozen decision is
        \texttt{PAPER C}: B3 $-$ B0 $=+0.25$ on SemanticKITTI with the CI containing zero, $+0.54$ on
        Occ3D. The semantic contribution over trivial nearest-evidence copying is not significant.
  \item \textbf{Semantics are teacher-bounded.} The 2D teacher signal is $63.9\,\%$ top-1 correct and
        covers $12.3\,\%$ of hidden occupied voxels. Oracle instance grouping moves the primary
        population by $+0.09$ mIoU (CI $[-0.27,+0.34]$); a VLM recognizer on the same regions moves it
        $+0.88$ (CI touching zero); region extension $\pm0.02$. Re-grouping or re-naming does not move
        the bound.
  \item \textbf{Both systems are zero-shot on both benchmarks.} OccAny trains on DDAD, ONCE, PandaSet,
        VKITTI and Waymo; we train on KITTI-360. Neither trains on SemanticKITTI or nuScenes.
  \item \textbf{Absolute numbers are below supervised methods} trained on these benchmarks. The claim is
        zero-shot and causal, not absolute state of the art.
  \item Open: OccAny 1-camera \texttt{5frames} semantics on Occ3D; a correct OccAny 5-frame causal variant.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Retracted}\label{sec:retracted}

Two figures produced on 15 October 2026 are withdrawn. A monkeypatch that reversed OccAny's input window
returned SC IoU $14.62$, which is $9.4$ points \emph{below} OccAny's own single-frame $24.03$ \nm
impossible, since a causal 5-frame window contains that single frame plus four more. Running OccAny's
unmodified 1-frame preset reproduced $24.02$, isolating the fault to the patch.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
retracted claim & corrected \\
\midrule
``the future window costs OccAny $2.69$ mIoU / $10.6$ SC IoU'' & $+1.91$ SC IoU, $+3.34$ SSC mIoU \\
``our geometry leads OccAny by $+11.8$ SC IoU'' & $-0.04$ to $+2.74$ (SK), $+4.46$ to $+17.36$ (Occ3D) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
